========================================================================
EDUCONNECT - APPLICATION RUN GUIDE & NEW FEATURES OVERVIEW
========================================================================

## 1. How the App Runs & Architecture
- **UI Framework:** Built entirely with Jetpack Compose and Material 3 design components.
- **Architecture:** Follows the MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel) architectural pattern with Google Hilt for dependency injection.
- **Backend & Persistence:** Powered by Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, backed by local Room database caching for complete offline functionality.
- **AI Integration:** Uses Retrofit and OkHttp REST clients to communicate with the Gemini API for intelligent tutoring, summaries, and adaptive quizzes.

---

## 2. Comprehensive List of New Features

### A. In-App Multilingual Support
- **Languages Supported:** English, isiZulu, Afrikaans, and Sesotho.
- **How it Works:** Managed via `LanguageManager` and `MainActivity.attachBaseContext` propagation. Changing the language updates app locales and instantly recreates the activity across all Android devices and manufacturers. AI features automatically adapt their prompts to respond in the selected language.

### B. Dark, Light, and System Theme Mode
- **Modes:** Light Theme, Dark Theme, and System Default.
- **How it Works:** Configured with Material3 DayNight palettes, managed by `ThemeManager`, persisted locally, and applied instantly on app startup and runtime selection.

### C. Offline Authentication & Room Caching
- **Offline Support:** If offline or network errors occur during login or registration, the app seamlessly falls back to the local Room database, ensuring learners can always access their accounts and study materials.

### D. Google Sign-In & Strict Registration
- **Account Chooser:** Tapping "Sign in with Google" prompts the system account picker (`AccountManager.newChooseAccountIntent`), letting users choose from any Gmail account on their device rather than auto-signing into the last one.
- **Security Rule:** Unregistered Gmail accounts cannot log in directly; users must register their accounts first. First-time Google sign-ups are guided to complete their profile registration.

### E. Persistent Login Session
- **Auto-Login:** When the app is closed and reopened, `MainActivity` checks existing authentication state (`FirebaseAuth.getInstance().currentUser`) and opens directly to the Dashboard, keeping users logged in across app restarts.

### F. Internal Storage File Uploads
- **Document Picker:** Students and teachers can upload any file (PDFs, Word documents, text files, images) from their device internal storage directly to any subject's material or assignment section.

### G. Randomized Quiz Engine
- **Subject Question Banks:** Each subject features a curated bank of 15 curriculum-aligned quiz questions.
- **Random Selection:** Every time a user enters or re-enters a quiz, the app randomly shuffles and presents 5 unique questions.

### H. Study Time & Subject Tracker Tab
- **Study Tracking:** A dedicated bottom navigation tab (`⏱️ Study`) that tracks study hours per subject.
- **Period Filter:** Switch dynamically between *This Week*, *This Month*, and *This Term* to view graphs, min/max study time goals, and adherence rates.
- **Gamification:** Unlockable study milestone badges (e.g., "Math Whiz", "7-Day Streak", "Exam Ready").

### I. Interactive Calendar View
- **Month/Year Navigation:** Users can navigate through previous and next months and years.
- **Visibility Toggle:** Easily show or hide the calendar view from the Notifications screen.
